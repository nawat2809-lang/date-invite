HOW IT WORKS - Black Hole
JIN2 · @jin_22ee

The whole artwork is one Python file. Every cell of the screen asks
"how hot is the gas here?" and the answer becomes a letter and a colour.

1. The circle
   A thin ring of light draws itself around an empty centre. That empty
   centre is the shadow: nothing behind it is drawn.

2. The glow
   A soft red glow swells and settles around the ring, made from a few
   layered sine waves that move slowly.

3. The disk
   The disk is a flat ring of gas seen almost edge on, tilted 17 degrees.
   Each cell is turned back into a radius and an angle on that disk.
   The gas is thin rings on circular orbits, and the inner ones turn
   faster (speed falls off with radius to the power 1.5).
   The side coming toward you is a little brighter.

4. The bent ring
   The far side of the disk is bent by gravity and shows up as the
   bright ring around the shadow. It uses the same rings of gas as the
   disk, so the streaks run on from one to the other.

5. The flash
   At 7.8 seconds everything flares, a shock ring runs outward and the
   view slowly moves in and looks down a little. After that, sparks of
   light ride the orbits along the disk and up over the shadow.

6. From heat to letters
   Hotter cells get denser letters (from : and ; up to 8 B $ #) and
   warmer colours (deep red, red, orange, amber, pale gold).
   The ring itself is drawn with / | \ - following its direction.
   Each frame fades into the next and a soft glow is added on top.

Things you can try (for yourself)
   TILT, INC       how the disk leans and how open it looks
   R0              size of the shadow
   R_IN, R_OUT     where the disk starts and ends
   FLASH           when the flash happens
   TAG_COLOR       the colours
