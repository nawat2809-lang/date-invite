HOW IT WORKS - Cat Knight
JIN2 · @jin_22ee

The kitten knight was drawn in letters from a photo that isn't mine,
so the photo is not included. Here is how the 600 frames were made,
and what cat_knight.py does with them.

How the frames were made
1. Cut out
   The knight was cut out of the photo and split into three layers:
   the knight, the cape, and a patch of grass under the feet.

2. The cape
   A 9 x 9 grid of points was laid over the cape and joined by springs.
   The points by the shoulder are pinned. Wind with gusts pushes the
   rest, ripples run from the shoulder to the hem, and the springs keep
   the cloth together.

3. Wind on the fur and the grass
   The fur on the edge of the head and the grass shift a few pixels with
   the same wind. The grass is brightest under the feet and darker
   toward its edge, like a spotlight.

4. From pixels to letters
   A camera starts on the face and slowly pulls back to the whole
   knight. Each frame was cut into a grid of 154 x 87 letter cells:
   - brightness picks the letter, from 1il!;: (dark) to 08B$#&%Q (bright),
   - the edge of the knight and strong lines inside it become | / \ - _,
   - the colour comes from the photo, the eyes stay dark.
   A light runs up the sword three times and the tip flashes.

What cat_knight.py does
5. Plays the frames
   For each frame it reads which letter, colour and brightness each cell
   has, draws the letters, lets the last frame fade behind it and adds
   a soft glow.

Things you can try (for yourself)
   CW, CH, FONT_PX   letter size
   TRAIL             how long the last frame stays behind
   COLORS            change the colours of the whole piece
