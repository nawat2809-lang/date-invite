HOW IT WORKS - Diamond Ring
JIN2 · @jin_22ee

The whole artwork is one Python file. It works in six steps,
and the sections inside diamond_ring.py follow the same numbers.

1. The ring
   The band, four prongs and a round brilliant diamond are built from
   tens of thousands of points in 3D space. Each point also knows which
   way its surface faces (its "normal").
   The diamond has 57 flat facets. Every point on one facet shares the
   same normal. That is why a whole facet lights up at once, like a
   real cut stone.

2. How it appears
   Every point gets a number for "when it is drawn": the bottom of the
   band first, up both sides, then the prongs, then the diamond from its
   tip. A bright point of light travels through those numbers and draws
   the ring as it goes.

3. Light and the details
   - A key light from above and a rim light that slowly circles the ring.
   - Brightness = how much a surface faces the light, plus a sharp
     highlight where light bounces straight back to us.
   - Facets that catch the light flicker between white and soft colours
     (diamond "fire").
   - Golden sparks fly off the drawing point and fall with gravity; a thin
     ring of light rushes outward the moment it is finished; two golden
     orbits and golden dust circle it.

4. The camera
   A still camera, a little above, like a jeweller's photograph.
   Every point is projected onto the screen with perspective.

5. From points to letters
   The screen is a grid of letter cells. For each cell:
   - the nearest point wins,
   - edges of each part become  | / \ - _  following the outline,
   - the inside becomes letters chosen by brightness (dim: 1 i l ; bright: 8 B $ #),
   - sparkling facets become  *  and the brightest throw four-pointed rays.

6. The window
   Each cell is drawn as a tiny glyph in its colour, a soft glow is added,
   and the picture updates up to 30 times a second.

Things you can try (for yourself)
   TURN    seconds for one full turn
   DRAW    when the ring starts and finishes appearing
   COLOR   the colour of each kind of point
   W, H    window size
